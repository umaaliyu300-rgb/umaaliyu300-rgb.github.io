Hidden Engine Publisher

A private publishing tool for the YouTube channel The Hidden Engine.

The Hidden Engine makes original documentaries and Shorts about business, money, technology, global companies, economics and industries.

Hidden Engine Publisher is the channel owner's own software. It runs on the owner's computer and uses the official YouTube Data API to upload finished videos, thumbnails and video details to that one channel, and to read basic channel information. It is not offered to the public, and it does not access anyone else's account.
